## 📷 Photography on a Cruise

### What did I do?
I shot photos of every band performing on a cruise that sailed from Africa up to Europe, mostly stopping in Portugal, Morocco and Spain. Along the way I became good friends with several of the musicians on board. They were all accomplished, very talented creatives, and photographing them every night meant I was working right alongside people at the top of their craft. It was also my first time in Morocco, which made the whole trip even more exciting.

### When did I do it?
Summer 2026.

### What was good about it?
I got to bring together two things I love, photography and music, and do them for real. Shooting live bands night after night pushed my skills fast: I had to deal with stage lighting, movement and timing, and I couldn't ask anyone to pose. I also built real friendships with musicians whose work I respect, and I got to see Portugal, Morocco and Spain with a camera in my hand.

### What was my favourite memory?
Two kinds of memories stand out. Away from the camera, my favourite part was every time we docked and I got off the ship to explore with the band. We walked through botanical gardens in Portugal, climbed mountains and saw castles, and wandered through Tangier, Morocco together. I'll always cherish that time. For the photography itself, my favourite night was when they played their biggest gig of the trip at The Manor, the main venue on board the Virgin Voyages ship. Capturing that show is the best work I've ever done.

### What didn't I enjoy?
Everyone in the bands was at least in their 30s, so there was a big age gap. We got along really well and had intellectual conversations that I valued a lot, but for the first few days I had serious imposter syndrome. They had all done so much with their lives, and part of me kept wondering whether they were just being nice and letting a kid hang out with them. I'm not even sure any of them knew how old I was, and none of them seemed to care. They thought I was interesting and valued what I brought with my work and my art. Figuring that out was really validating. The schedule was also very long and very tiring. It's not something I could do in a healthy way long term.

### Grade: A-
An experience I'd happily do again, and the length was perfect. Any longer and I might have lost my mind!
