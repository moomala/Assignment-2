## Photography on a Cruise

### What did I do?
I was the photographer for all the bands on a cruise from Africa to Europe. We stopped in Portugal, Morocco and Spain, and I became good friends with a lot of the musicians.

### When did I do it?
Summer 2026.

### What was good about it?
- I got to combine photography and music, my two favourite things
- Shooting live shows every night made me a lot better at photography
- I saw Morocco for the first time

### What was my favourite memory?
Exploring each city with the band when we got off the ship: botanical gardens in Portugal, castles, mountains, and Tangier in Morocco. For photos, my favourite night was their biggest show at The Manor on the Virgin Voyages ship.

### What didn't I enjoy?
The musicians were all in their 30s, so for the first few days I had some imposter syndrome and felt a bit out of place. Once I realized they valued me and my work, that went away. The days were also really long and tiring.

### Grade: A-
I'd do it again, but it was the perfect length. Any longer and I would've lost my mind.
