## 🎨 Visual Arts at Canterbury High School of the Arts

### What did I do?
I spent four years in Canterbury High School of the Arts' specialized visual arts program, working in photography, printmaking, sculpture, ceramics, textiles, fashion, painting, drawing, figure drawing from live models, and murals. The program also took me on trips to France and Barcelona. That's the base my eye for design comes from, and the reason I'm studying UX now.

### When did I do it?
2021 to 2025 (Grades 9–12). I graduated in 2025.

### What was good about it?
It's had the biggest impact on my life of anything I've done. I grew up fast there and learned skills and had experiences I couldn't have gotten anywhere else. Trying almost every medium taught me how to think visually and work hard on long projects. I think it's the reason I succeed the way I do, and why I'm as mature as I am today.

### What was my favourite memory?
Painting a mural in my Grade 12 year. I spent 12 hours a day in the studio, from 10 a.m. to 10 p.m., painting alongside my peers. It was bittersweet: it was one of the first times I got to share a class with all my fellow visual artists, and it was also the last class we'd ever have together at that school. Four years of intensive arts learning all came down to that final project. The mural now hangs in the school and will be there for years to come, and I'm really proud of it.

### What didn't I enjoy?
Honestly, the hardest part was that it ended. My favourite project was also the last class I'd ever have there with my fellow artists, and leaving a community like that was bittersweet. ✏️ *(add anything else you didn't enjoy, e.g. workload or long studio hours)*

The most formative experience of my life. I don't know where I'd be without it.
