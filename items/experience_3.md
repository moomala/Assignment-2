## Visual Arts at Canterbury High School

### What did I do?
I was in the visual arts program at Canterbury High School of the Arts. I did photography, printmaking, sculpture, ceramics, textiles, fashion, painting, drawing and murals. I also went to France and Barcelona with the program.

### When did I do it?
2021 to 2025. I graduated in 2025.

### What was good about it?
- It's had the biggest impact on my life of anything
- I grew up a lot and learned skills I couldn't have gotten anywhere else
- It's the reason I'm in UX design today

### What was my favourite memory?
Painting a mural in Grade 12. We were in the studio from 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. with all the other visual arts students. It was our first and last class all together. The mural still hangs at the school and I'm really proud of it.

### What didn't I enjoy?
The school was a protected bubble. It was really inclusive, but it didn't prepare us for the real world. It was hard to watch my friends get shell-shocked after graduation.

### Grade: A+
The most important experience of my life.
