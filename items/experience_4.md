## 📚 Peer Tutoring

### What did I do?
I was a peer tutor and student teacher for a Grade 9 French geography class. I helped students understand the course material, and I had to explain things clearly in a second language.

### When did I do it?
Grade 11 (2023–2024 school year).

### What was good about it?
It was really rewarding. I learned a lot about myself, including how much I enjoy working in education. Watching a class relax and start to trust each other, and knowing I helped make that happen, showed me I'm good at building community and making people feel comfortable.

### What was my favourite memory?
The first few times I really connected with the students. The teacher I worked with was very hard on the class, and everyone felt like they were walking on eggshells. Over the year, I helped turn that tense room into a little family. I'm still in touch with some of those students. They graduate this year, and I'll be at their grad show to support them.

### What didn't I enjoy?
It was very hard work. The workload was intense, and I didn't always get a thank-you for the effort I put in. Working under a teacher who was so tough on the students also made the classroom stressful, especially at the start.

### Grade: B+
Very rewarding, but demanding and not always appreciated.
