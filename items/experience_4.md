## Peer Tutoring

### What did I do?
I was a peer tutor for a Grade 9 French geography class.

### When did I do it?
Grade 11.

### What was good about it?
- It was really rewarding
- I learned that I enjoy working in education
- I helped turn the class into a team

### What was my favourite memory?
Connecting with the students. The teacher was really hard on them, and everyone was walking on eggshells. By the end of the year, the class felt like a little family. I still talk to some of them, and I'm going to their grad show this year.

### What didn't I enjoy?
It was a lot of work, and I didn't always get a thank-you.

### Grade: B+
