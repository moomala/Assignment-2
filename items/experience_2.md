## Guitar

### What did I do?
I've played guitar since I was little. My first one was a plastic acoustic from Toys R Us, but I wanted to play electric. When my teacher quit, I stopped for a few years. I got back into it in Grade 5 and I've played at least an hour a day ever since.

### When did I do it?
Since I was a kid, and seriously since Grade 5. I still play every day.

### What was good about it?
- It's been my biggest hobby for years
- It taught me discipline
- It's been with me through a lot

### What was my favourite memory?
Getting my first Manson guitar in Grade 11. Manson is owned by Matt Bellamy from Muse, my favourite band. The first time I played it, something clicked and it felt like I was finally fluent in guitar. I now own one of their handmade guitars.

### What didn't I enjoy?
I get frustrated a lot. I improved really fast from Grade 12 to first year, but now I hit walls and get stuck for a long time. Not seeing progress can make me less motivated to play.
