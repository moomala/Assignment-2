## 🎸 Playing Guitar

### What did I do?
I've played guitar since I was very young. My first guitar was a plastic acoustic from Toys R Us, but I figured out quickly that electric was what I wanted to play. Finding a teacher who would teach electric guitar to a kid that young was really hard, and when my teacher quit, I stopped playing for a few years. Around Grade 5, I fell back in love with it, and I've played every day since, at least an hour a day. Today my main guitar is a Cort MBM-1, and I haven't wanted to go back to any other brand. I got hooked on that Manson / Matt Bellamy style through Muse.

### When did I do it?
I started as a young kid and came back seriously around Grade 5. I've played every day since then and still do.

### What was good about it?
It's the most consistent thing in my life. Practising for at least an hour every day has taught me discipline, and I can see that it pays off over time. Guitar is my creative outlet, and it has connected me with people who love music as much as I do.
