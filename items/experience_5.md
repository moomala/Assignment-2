## Travelling

### What did I do?
I've been to Africa, Asia, South America, North America and Europe. Australia is the only continent I haven't been to yet.

### When did I do it?
My whole life. My biggest solo trip was backpacking the UK when I was 18.

### What was good about it?
- Seeing different cultures and a lot of history
- Becoming more independent
- Visiting places connected to music I love

### What was my favourite memory?
Backpacking the UK on my own. I stayed in Camden, the punk capital of the world, then took the train to Exeter. From there I visited Wales, Stonehenge, Plymouth and Teignmouth, which is Muse's hometown. I also saw the Cavern in Exeter, where Muse got their start.

### What didn't I enjoy?
When I was younger I travelled with my mom. I love her, but after two months together 24/7, we'd start to get sick of each other near the end.
