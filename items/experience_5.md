## 🌍 Travelling the World

### What did I do?

### When did I do it?
Throughout my life. My biggest solo trip was backpacking the UK at 18, and I went back to the UK most recently after the cruise in summer 2026.

### What was my favourite memory?
Backpacking around the UK on my own when I was 18. It was my first time there, and I'd always wanted to go. I started with a few days in Camden Town in London, which is basically the punk capital of the world. I'm big into punk music and fashion, and my favourite shoe brand, New Rock, is everywhere there, even though it's really hard to find in North America. Then I took a beautiful train ride to Exeter and stayed for a couple of weeks. It made a central base for Wales, Stonehenge, Plymouth and Teignmouth. Exeter is also home to the Cavern, the venue where Muse got their start. The biggest moment for me was visiting Teignmouth, Muse's hometown: a small, beautiful town right on the coast.
